Posts

This is where you manage all your blog posts - create, edit, preview, translate, and delete.

🚀 Getting Started

Your published and draft posts appear here. Use the status filter to find posts needing review. Click New Post to start a fresh article, or go to Planned Posts to create from your content calendar.

Post Workflow States

  • Draft - Work in progress, not visible to visitors
  • In Review - Ready for review before publishing
  • Published - Live on the public blog
  • Archived - Removed from public view but preserved

Top Buttons

  • New Post (orange, top right) - Creates a blank French (fr-FR) draft and opens the Blog Editor immediately. You can change the language in the editor if needed.
  • Manage Tags - Opens a dialog where you can add new tags, rename existing ones, or delete them.

Filters

Use the filter card at the top to narrow your list:

  • Status - Show All, Draft, In Review, Published, or Archived.
  • Language - All Languages, English, or French.
  • The post count shows how many posts match your filters.

Post Cards

Each post shows: title, description (2 lines), badges (language, status, tags, Featured).

Action Icons (right side)

Every post card has 5 action icons. From left to right:

  1. Status dropdown - Click the status badge to cycle through Draft, In Review, Published, Archived without opening the editor.
  2. Eye icon - Preview the post exactly as it appears on the public blog. Opens in a new tab.
  3. Globe/translation icon - Create a translated copy in the other language. All images and structure are preserved - just translate the text.
  4. Pencil icon - Open the post in the Blog Editor for full editing.
  5. Trash icon (red) - Delete the post permanently. You'll be asked to confirm.

Public Blog Post Features

  • Share buttons - Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Copy Link
  • Featured Articles - Related posts shown after the article
  • Newsletter signup - Email subscription form

💡 Tips

  • Click New Post to create a French draft, then use AI Assist in the editor to generate content. Change language in the editor if needed.
  • To create from a content plan, go to Planned Posts and click "Create Post".
  • The Translation button copies everything. Just replace the text.
  • Change post status directly from the list - no need to open the editor.
