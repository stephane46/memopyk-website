Geographic Market Analysis

See where your website visitors come from around the world. This helps you understand your global reach and focus on key markets.

Market Intelligence Cards

Four summary cards at the top:

  • Top Market - Country with the most sessions (shown with flag)
  • Market Coverage - Total number of countries reached
  • Best Engagement - Country with highest return rate (rates above 100% mean repeat visits)
  • Global Reach - Total unique visitors worldwide

Interactive Map

The world map uses color intensity to show visitor volume:

  • Dark blue = High traffic countries
  • Light blue = Medium traffic countries
  • Gray = No visitors from this country

Hover over any country to see a tooltip with:

  • Country name
  • Number of sessions
  • Number of visitors
  • Engagement percentage

Use your mouse to zoom and pan around the map. Click the Reset button to return to the original position.

Top Markets Table

The ranked list shows your top 10 countries by sessions. Each row displays:

  • Rank (numbered 1-10)
  • Country name with flag
  • Sessions count
  • Visitors count
  • Engagement rate (percentage)

Country Distribution Charts

Two pie charts break down your traffic:

  • Sessions by Country - Total visits from top 8 countries
  • Visitors by Country - Unique people from top 8 countries

Hover over pie slices to see exact numbers and percentages. The legend shows country names with color codes.

Tips

  • Compare Sessions vs Visitors to find countries with repeat visits
  • High engagement rates suggest strong interest from that market
  • Use geographic data to plan language targeting (French vs English)
  • Click the Export button to download detailed country data
