Analytics Overview

Your main website performance metrics at a glance � visitor counts, traffic trends, and real-time activity.

?? Getting Started

Check your Sessions count for the last 7 days to see recent traffic. The percentage arrow below each number compares to the previous period � green up means growth, red down means decline. Switch between GA4 and MEMOPYK to compare total vs. filtered traffic.

Data Source Toggle

Switch between GA4 (Google Analytics, all traffic) and MEMOPYK (internal tracking, filtered to exclude your admin visits). The orange badge shows which source is active.

?? Filters

Three filters above the analytics tabs narrow the data:

  • Language � ALL, EN, or FR buttons filter visitors by browser language. The active filter is highlighted in orange.
  • Country � The green dropdown filters by visitor country (e.g., France, Canada, United States). Set to "ALL" by default.
  • IP Filtered � The red badge next to the data source toggle means your excluded IPs (configured in the Exclusions tab) are being filtered out, so you see only real visitor traffic. The IPs: N badge in the header shows how many IPs are excluded.

Visitor Overview

Three summary cards display key visitor metrics:

  • Sessions � Visitor sessions. The card title shows the active data source (e.g., "Sessions (GA4)" or "Sessions (MEMOPYK)").
  • Unique Visitors � Distinct visitors during the selected period.
  • Return Visitors � Visitors who came back more than once.

Click the eye icon at the bottom of each card to expand a detailed session log � individual visits listed with columns: Location (with country flag), Language, Visit Time, IP Address, and Duration.

Active Users Badge

The green badge at the top right shows how many visitors are currently on your site, updated every 15 seconds. A pulsing green dot means live tracking is active.

Analytics Report

Below the cards, a summary box provides a plain-language report for the current view � total sessions, unique visitors, and returning visitor percentage.

?? Tips

  • Check Sessions weekly to spot traffic trends � a sudden drop may signal a site issue
  • Use the Language filter to compare French vs. English traffic and plan content priorities
  • Compare GA4 and MEMOPYK numbers � a big gap means many visits are admin traffic being filtered out
  • The Active Users badge catches traffic spikes in real time, useful during campaigns
