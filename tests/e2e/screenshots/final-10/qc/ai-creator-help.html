AI Creator

Generate blog posts using AI, powered by Brand Brain context. The AI writes in MEMOPYK's voice using your brand guidelines.

🚀 Getting Started

Choose a topic from Planned Posts or type your own, set the language to English or French, and click Generate with AI. The AI will create a complete article in 15-30 seconds. Review the content, make any edits, then click Save Blog Post to add it to your Posts list.

Example Workflow

  1. Enter a topic like "The Ultimate Guide to Digitizing Old Photos in 2025"
  2. Select English (en-US) from the Language dropdown
  3. Add your primary keyword: "photo digitization"
  4. Add secondary keywords: "memory preservation, family photos, photo scanning"
  5. Set Target Word Count to 900 (or adjust as needed)
  6. Click Generate with AI and wait 15-30 seconds
  7. Review the generated article - the AI creates title, slug, meta description, and formatted HTML content
  8. Click Save Blog Post to add it to your Posts (status: Draft)

Step 1: Configure

Fill in the following fields:

  • Topic - Describe what the blog post should be about (required). Be specific for better results.
  • Language - French (FR) or English (EN). The entire article will be written in this language.
  • Published At (optional) - Choose a publish date or leave blank to use current date/time.
  • Primary Keyword (optional) - The main SEO keyword to target. Example: "photo digitization"
  • Secondary Keywords (optional) - Additional keywords (comma-separated). Example: "memory preservation, family photos, photo scanning"
  • Target Word Count - Target length (default 900 words). The AI aims for this length but may vary slightly.
  • Notes (optional) - Any special instructions or angles for the AI. Example: "Focus on families with young children"

Click Generate with AI to start. Generation takes 15-30 seconds.

Step 2: Review & Save

The AI generates a complete article with:

  • Title, slug, and meta description optimized for SEO
  • Formatted HTML content with headings, paragraphs, and image placeholders
  • SEO metadata and suggested tags based on your keywords

The generated content appears in a preview area below the form. Review the content, make any adjustments, then click Save Blog Post to add it to your Posts list with Draft status.

How Brand Brain Works

The AI reads all your Brand Brain entries (brand identity, tone, writing rules, audience, SEO guidelines) to generate content that matches MEMOPYK's voice. Edit Brand Brain in the AI Context tab to improve results.

💡 Tips

  • Be specific with your topic for better results. "Tips for preserving family memories" is better than just "memories".
  • Always review and edit AI content before publishing. The AI is a starting point, not the final draft.
  • Use the Blog Editor's AI Assist for quick in-editor generation when you need to expand or rewrite specific sections.
  • Start with Draft status and review before publishing. You can change status later in the Posts tab.
  • Update your Brand Brain entries to improve AI output quality over time.
